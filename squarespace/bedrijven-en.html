How it works

  1. Send us your request: number of employees, start date, budget and preferred locations.
  2. We call you within one working day to discuss the requirements.
  3. We propose suitable homes from our listings and network.
  4. Viewings with the employee, in person or by video.
  5. Rental agreement in the employee’s name or, if the landlord agrees, in the company’s name.
  6. Key handover with an inspection report and a walk-through of the home.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost us as an employer?

For homes we offer on behalf of a landlord, neither the tenant nor the employer pays agency fees. If you need help beyond our listings, we will discuss the options with you first.

Can the rental agreement be in our company’s name?

That is possible if the landlord agrees. Often the agreement is in the employee’s name and the employer pays all or part of the rent.

Do you have furnished homes?

Yes, a large part of our rental listings is furnished or upholstered. Let us know in your request what your employee needs.

Do you work with relocation agencies?

Yes. We are happy to coordinate viewings and the contract with your relocation agency, so your employee has one smooth process.

Which areas do you cover?

Mainly Haarlem, Heemstede, Bloemendaal, Zandvoort, Hoofddorp and the surrounding area, and through our other offices also Amsterdam, Amstelveen and Leiden.
