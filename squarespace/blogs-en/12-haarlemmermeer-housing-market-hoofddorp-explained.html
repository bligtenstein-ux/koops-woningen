Anyone looking at the Dutch housing market quickly sees one national picture: more supply, less frenzy and moderate price growth. But anyone who looks at the market locally sees that the differences can be large.

The Haarlemmermeer region is a good example. Because of its location, its housing stock and its strong links with Amsterdam and Schiphol, the local housing market has its own dynamics.

The strength of the location

Haarlemmermeer lies in the middle of the Amsterdam Metropolitan Area and has a strong economic pull. Amsterdam and Schiphol provide a lot of jobs, while places such as Hoofddorp, Nieuw-Vennep and Badhoevedorp are attractive to people who are looking for space but want to stay connected to the big city.

That combination creates structural demand for homes.

The municipality of Haarlemmermeer accordingly describes the region as an economic magnet. Even though many homes have been built in recent years, demand remains high. Supply is limited particularly in the affordable and mid-priced segments.

Lots of family homes, but also lots of demand

A striking feature of Haarlemmermeer is the make-up of its housing stock. Around 74% of homes are single-family houses. That is relatively high.

This makes the region attractive to families and to people moving up the ladder who want to move from Amsterdam or from an apartment to a more spacious home.

At the same time, this creates a challenge. Someone who wants to downsize from a family home, for example, does not always have a suitable alternative. As a result, homes stay occupied for longer and movement through the market comes under pressure.

Greater Amsterdam moves differently from the Netherlands as a whole

The figures clearly show why the national average is not always a good basis for judging the local market.

In the second quarter of 2026, 4,345 homes were sold in the Greater Amsterdam region, 4.7% more than a year earlier. Nationally, the number of sales rose by 6.6%.

Price development also differs. The average transaction price in Greater Amsterdam came to €577,000, a fall of 0.4% compared with a year earlier. Nationally, by contrast, the average transaction price rose by 2.1%.

That does not mean homes in Haarlemmermeer are suddenly becoming cheaper. Above all, it shows that the market responds differently to changes in supply, demand and types of home.

Not every home sells in the same way

Within Haarlemmermeer, too, the differences are large. A well-maintained family home in an attractive location can still attract a lot of interest, while a home that matches current demand less well may stay on the market longer.

That requires sellers to do more than just look at the average house price.

The right questions are:

  • Which homes are competing with mine?
  • How much comparable supply is currently for sale?
  • What is buyer demand like in my specific neighbourhood?
  • What price fits the current market?
  • How do I make my home stand out from comparable homes?

It is precisely at local level that these factors can make a big difference.

Why a local sales strategy matters

The Haarlemmermeer housing market shows why a national average is only a starting point.

The market is influenced by its location between Amsterdam and Schiphol, the large stock of family homes, the limited availability of affordable homes and the specific demand from families and people moving up the ladder.

For sellers, this means that a good sales strategy starts with knowledge of the local market.

Not every home needs the same approach. A home that can expect a lot of interest calls for a different strategy from a home where price, presentation or timing are especially important.

The key conclusion

The Dutch housing market actually consists of dozens of local markets. What applies nationally therefore does not necessarily apply one-to-one to Haarlemmermeer.

Especially in a region with a strong economic position and a specific housing stock, demand for good homes remains high, even when the national market cools somewhat.

So if you want to sell your home in Haarlemmermeer, it is wise not just to look at the national housing market, but above all at what is happening right now in your own street, neighbourhood and municipality.
