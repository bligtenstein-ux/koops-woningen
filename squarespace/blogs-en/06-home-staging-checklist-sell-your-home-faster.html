Selling your house? Then naturally you want your home to catch the attention of potential buyers straight away. A good first impression can make a big difference. But how do you make sure your home is presented at its best before it goes on the market?

With good preparation you make your house more attractive to interested buyers and increase your chances of a successful sale. This practical checklist will help you get started!

1. Declutter and create space

A tidy home looks bigger and calmer. Put personal belongings away, remove unnecessary furniture and make sure kitchen worktops are clear and cupboards are tidy. This helps potential buyers imagine how they would furnish the home themselves.

2. Give it a thorough clean

A clean house immediately looks well cared for. Pay extra attention to the kitchen, bathroom, windows and floors. Do not forget the less obvious spots either. A fresh home shows that the house has been well looked after.

3. Fix small defects

Small maintenance jobs can already make a big difference. Think of replacing broken light bulbs, repairing damage to walls or fixing loose skirting boards. You do not need to undertake a major renovation; it is precisely the small improvements that help create a well-kept look.

4. Create a neutral, welcoming atmosphere

Not every buyer has the same taste. So wherever possible, choose calm colours and subtle accessories. Temporarily remove eye-catching personal decorations and let in plenty of daylight. This gives the home an inviting feel.

5. Do not forget the outdoor space

The first impression starts at the front door. Make sure the garden is neat, the patio is clean and the entrance looks well kept. A balcony deserves attention too. An attractive outdoor space can be just that extra plus point for potential buyers.

6. Prepare your home for the photos

The photos are often the first thing buyers see of your home. So make sure every room is neat and tidy when the photographer comes. Professional property photography combined with a good presentation makes sure your house looks its best online.

7. Gather important documents

Good preparation goes beyond how your home looks. Make sure important documents are available, such as the energielabel (energy label), building drawings, maintenance information and any VvE (homeowners' association) documents. This prevents unnecessary delays during the sales process.

Do you need to completely renovate your home before selling?

Certainly not! Many sellers think a new kitchen or bathroom is necessary to make their home attractive. That is often not the case at all. Small adjustments, a good clean and a well-kept presentation are often enough.

So always discuss with your selling agent which improvements are actually worthwhile for your home. That way you avoid unnecessary costs.

Good preparation makes the difference

Getting a home ready for sale does not have to be complicated. With a few smart adjustments you make sure your house makes a good first impression and is presented attractively to potential buyers.
